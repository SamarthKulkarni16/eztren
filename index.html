Eztren

A global debate sport, ranked in letters, not numbers.

Visit eztren.xyz →

Eztren is a live debate platform. Two players argue a topic, a judge scores the match in real time, and every round is recorded — video, transcript, and an AI-generated summary — building a public, searchable archive of debate and reasoning over time.

Players climb the alphabet leagues, starting near Z and working their way up toward becoming A, the top rank in the game. The project started under the name One Alphabet, which is still visible in some of the league names.

Note on the name: Eztren is a debate sport platform at eztren.xyz. It is unrelated to Honeywell's "eZtrend" industrial data recorder software — different product, different industry, similar-sounding name only.
